# Board call, 1 October 2026

Speakers: Margaret Hale (chair of trustees), Priya Shah (volunteer coordinator), Sam Okafor (product lead).

**Margaret:** Before we talk about the app, I want to be clear about what worries the board. It is not the no-shows. The real problem is that our volunteer base is shrinking and ageing.

**Priya:** The numbers are stark. 60% of our volunteers are over 65. New sign-ups have halved this year compared with last year.

**Margaret:** If that carries on, we will not have enough people to open the doors in two or three years, whatever the app does.

**Sam:** What about the no-shows?

**Priya:** The Saturday no-shows come from a small group of over-stretched regulars. They do three or four shifts a week and some weeks they simply burn out. It is not the casual volunteers letting us down.

**Margaret:** So the app has to help us keep the regulars from burning out and make it easy for new, younger people to start with a single shift.

**Sam:** That changes how I frame next year. I'll draft something for the board.
