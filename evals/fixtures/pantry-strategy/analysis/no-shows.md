# No-show analysis, June to August 2026

Prepared by Sam Okafor from the coordinator's spreadsheet.

## Counting rule

A shift counts as a no-show when the volunteer neither attends nor swaps 24 hours ahead. Cancellations made more than 24 hours ahead are excluded from the count.

## Result

412 shifts ran from June to August. 74 were no-shows, which is 18%.

| Day | No-show rate |
|---|---|
| Saturday | 31% |
| Monday | 12% |
| Tuesday | 13% |
| Wednesday | 15% |
| Thursday | 12% |
| Friday | 14% |

## Reminder options

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Email reminder | Free | Already planned for every shift |
| SMS reminder | £40 a month | Not in the current budget |
| Phone call from the shift lead | 2 hours of lead time a week | Personal; the lead already knows most regulars |

The analysis does not say which option would cut no-shows most. No pilot has run yet.
